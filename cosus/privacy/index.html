Privacy policy

Cosus: Gym & Workout Planner (Android) · Effective 27 October 2026

In short

  • Your workouts, plans and body measurements are stored only on your phone. We have no servers and no copy of them.
  • There is no account. We don't collect your name or e-mail through the app.
  • The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers to show and measure ads. In the EU/UK you choose whether ads are personalised.
  • Your training and body data are never shared with advertisers.
  • The optional AI planner sends your plan request to the AI company you pick, using your own key.

Who we are

Cosus is made by Lusomaniac Games, Portugal. Contact: support@lusomaniac-games.xyz.

Data that stays on your phone

Everything you enter is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • the name you give your profile and the body figure you pick for exercise diagrams;
  • training plans, workouts, sets, weights, reps, timers, cardio and notes;
  • body measurements you log (such as weight, body fat, waist and resting heart rate);
  • photos or videos you attach to exercises;
  • settings, including language, sounds and the number of ad-free days you have.

We never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. Backups are only made when you choose to: the app creates a file, and you decide where it goes (for example Google Drive or e-mail). Backups never include AI keys.

AI planner (optional)

To have a plan written by AI, you add your own API key from one of these providers: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google (Gemini), xAI or OpenRouter. The key is encrypted on your phone and never sent to us.

When you ask for a plan, the app sends directly to that provider:

  • your goal, the plan length and training days;
  • the working weights it knows, and a summary of your recent training;
  • body measurements you have logged;
  • any notes you type for the plan;
  • the list of exercises the plan may use.

What the provider does with it is covered by its privacy policy and terms. If you choose to route requests through a Cosus server you run yourself, they pass through that server without being stored.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner on some screens and, only if you choose it in Settings, a video that gives you ad-free days. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information;
  • how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice when you first open the app. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad-free days › Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.

We never pass your workouts, body measurements or other health and fitness data to Google or any other ad company, and we don't use them to target ads. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Problem reports and e-mail

If you choose E-mail support in Settings › Help & diagnostics, the app opens your own e-mail app with a report attached. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. The report contains:

  • recent errors and failed requests;
  • the app version and device details (model, Android version, screen size);
  • your description of the problem.

It never includes API keys, passwords, e-mail addresses or workout notes. When you e-mail us we receive your e-mail address and message. We use them only to help you, and delete them within 12 months.

Permissions

  • Notifications: the ongoing workout notification and training reminders.
  • Notification access (optional, only if you turn on music controls): lets the app show and control the song playing in your music app. The app reads only the current track, and nothing leaves your phone.
  • Internet: for the AI planner (when you use it) and for ads.
  • Foreground service and keeping the screen on: so rest timers keep counting and ring while you train.

Children

Cosus is meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Legal basis and your rights

We don't process the data stored on your phone; you control it there directly. Ads rely on your consent where the law requires it, and otherwise on our legitimate interest in funding the free app. Support e-mails rely on your request.

Under the GDPR you may ask us for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data we hold, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If the change is significant, we'll also mention it in the app.
