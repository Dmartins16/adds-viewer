Cosus

A gym and workout planner that plans your weeks, times your rests and shows your progress. Everything is stored on your phone: no account needed.

Coming soon to Google Play Privacy policy

What it does

  • Multi-week plans from templates, or written for you by the AI model of your choice, using your own API key.
  • Log workouts fast: it jumps to your next set, and rest timers ring when it's time to go.
  • Timers built in: rest countdowns, timed holds and stretches, Tabata intervals.
  • Progress: estimated one-rep max per lift, body weight, personal records.
  • Phone extras: a workout notification with buttons, a floating rest timer, a home-screen widget and music controls.
  • Backups to a file whenever you want.
  • In English and Portuguese.

Support

Questions or problems: support@lusomaniac-games.xyz. In the app, Settings › Help & diagnostics › E-mail support attaches a problem report for you.
